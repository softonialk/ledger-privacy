Ledger – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · App: Ledger (com.softonia.ledger) · Developer: Softonia

Ledger is a personal expense tracker for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your phone. Ledger has no server and no user accounts in the cloud.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Account details – the name and email address you enter when you create a local account, and a salted hash of your password. These are used only to show your profile and to sign you in on this device. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Financial records – transactions, categories, wallets, budgets and notes you enter, plus any receipt photos you attach. These are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Preferences – currency, language, theme, budget-alert and app-lock settings.

Android's standard app backup may include this data if you have device backup enabled; it is encrypted by Google as part of your device backup and we cannot access it.

2. Optional AI features (Anthropic Claude)

Two features send data off your device, and only when you use them:

  • "Just type it" – the sentence you type (for example “lunch 850”) and the names of your categories are sent to the Anthropic Claude API so it can fill in the amount, category and date.
  • "Scan bill" – the receipt photo you choose and the names of your categories are sent to the Anthropic Claude API so it can read the merchant, total and date.

Nothing is sent automatically. Your account details, wallet balances and transaction history are not included. Anthropic processes this data under its own privacy policy; by default Anthropic does not use API inputs to train its models. If you never use these features, no data leaves your device.

3. Export

“Export & backup” creates a CSV file of your transactions and opens the Android share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice (for example an email or a cloud drive you pick).

4. Permissions

  • Internet – only for the optional Claude features above.
  • Photos – only when you choose a receipt image, through the system photo picker; Ledger never browses your gallery.
  • Biometrics / screen lock – only if you turn on App lock, to unlock the app. Ledger never sees your fingerprint; Android handles the check.

5. What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your bank, SMS or contacts.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ledger, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes everything the app stored. Because there is no server, there is nothing else to delete.

7. Children

Ledger is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
